Limited-resource strategy

Arcade Mode

Start with 16 coins. Each turn, pick 1 of 2 random buildings. Build wisely—when your coins run out, the game ends. Can you build a high-scoring city before you run dry?

Build at your own pace

Free Play Mode

Unlimited coins. Build any building anywhere. Your city expands as you place buildings on the edge. Manage profit and upkeep each turn—survive 20 losing turns and it's game over. No time pressure, just strategy.

Unpredictable events and limited resources

Challenges

Face unpredictable city events and adapt your strategy to survive. Challenges uses Arcade rules, but after every 5 turns all building costs increase by 1 coin, and taxes, disasters, and temporary economic effects can change your plans after turn 5.
