Welcome, City Planner

Read First

Ngee Ann City hands you an empty grid and a stack of building permits. Every tile you place changes the value of the tiles around it — the whole game is adjacency arithmetic dressed up as a skyline.

Three zoning charters govern the city: Arcade, a tight resource sprint; Challenge, an event-driven survival variant of Arcade; and Free Play, a slower economic simulation. Pick a charter from the legend above, or jump straight to a building's scoring rules.

Arcade and Challenge also share rotating Bonus Tasks that can award extra score or coins when you finish them before they expire.

Every building is colour-coded the same way in the manual as it is on the board — match the swatch, learn the rule.

Arcade Mode

16 Coins
  • Start with 16 coins on a 20×20 grid.
  • Each turn, the office offers you 1 of 2 randomly drawn building types — you don't get to pick freely.
  • Your first placement can go anywhere. Every placement after that must be adjacent to an existing building.
  • Placing or demolishing a building costs 1 coin.
  • The city keeps generating coin income each turn based on what's built.
  • Game over when the board is completely full, or when you run out of coins.

Because every tile is forced by the draw, Arcade rewards planning two moves ahead: know where an Industry tile would hurt your Residential score before it's offered to you. Bonus Tasks can also appear during Arcade, giving you timed side goals for extra coins or score.

Challenge Mode

Unpredictable
  • Challenge uses the same 20×20 grid, adjacency placement rule, and random 1 of 2 building draw as Arcade.
  • You start with 16 coins, but building placement cost rises over time. It starts at 1 coin and increases by 1 more every 5 turns.
  • After turn 5, random city events can begin. Events do not happen every turn, and there is a short cooldown between them.
  • Events can be immediate, scheduled, or temporary. Scheduled events warn you first, then trigger a few turns later. Temporary events last for several turns before ending.
  • Challenge ends when the board is full or when you no longer have enough coins to pay the next placement cost.
!Economic events: Construction Shortage raises building costs for a few turns, Economic Recession cuts coin income, and Maintenance Surge increases upkeep.
!Disasters: Earthquakes and fires can destroy buildings immediately, while tornadoes are announced in advance and strike a few turns later.
+Positive events: Emergency Relief Grants can award extra coins when they appear.

Challenge also includes milestone rewards such as building enough Residential tiles or reaching a larger city size, so smart survival and city growth both matter.

Bonus Tasks

Timed Goals
  • Bonus Tasks appear in Arcade and Challenge.
  • You receive them in batches of 5. A new batch appears only after every task in the current batch has been completed or expired.
  • Each task has a turn limit, a progress tracker, and a reward such as bonus score or bonus coins.
  • Progress is based on your real board state, so tasks can count total buildings of one type, mixed city growth, or the size of your largest connected Road network.
  • Completed tasks stay in the completed list, while expired tasks end with no reward.

Common Task Types

RBuilding-count tasks: examples include building 5 Residential in 8 turns, 3 Industry in 6 turns, 4 Parks in 7 turns, or 4 Commercial in 8 turns.
*Road tasks: some missions ask for a connected Road network of at least 6 or 12 tiles.
MixMixed tasks: later goals may ask for balanced Industry and Commercial growth, or at least 3 each of Residential, Industry, Commercial, and Park.

Use the in-game Bonus Tasks panel to switch between active and completed goals, watch the progress bar, and decide when a reward is worth changing your building plan.

Free Play Mode

Unlimited
  • Unlimited coins. Build any type, anywhere, from the very first turn.
  • Starts small — a 5×5 grid.
  • Building on a border cell expands the grid outward, up to two expansions total.
  • Every turn, the office calculates city-wide profit and upkeep (see the Economy figures below).
  • If upkeep outpaces profit for 20 consecutive turns, the city is declared insolvent and the game ends.

Upkeep & Profit Ledger

R+1 profit each. Every connected Residential cluster costs 1 upkeep, regardless of its size.
I+2 profit, −1 upkeep, per building.
C+3 profit, −2 upkeep, per building.
ONo profit. −1 upkeep per Park.
*No profit. Any Road segment that isn't connected to another Road costs −1 upkeep.

Residential (R)

Neighbourly

If adjacent to any Industry: the building is unhappy about the noise. It scores a flat 1 point total, no matter what else surrounds it.

Otherwise: it scores +1 for every adjacent Residential or Commercial tile, and +2 for every adjacent Park.

Industry (I)

City-wide

Industry doesn't care about its neighbours. Every Industry tile scores +1 for every other Industry tile in the entire city — placement is about volume, not location.

Trade-off: stacking Industry maximises its own score but poisons any nearby Residential down to 1 point.

Commercial (C)

Clustered

Each Commercial tile scores +1 for every adjacent Commercial tile. Shopping districts reward clustering.

Park (O)

Green Space

Each Park scores +1 for every adjacent Park tile. Its bigger contribution is indirect: it's the only tile that gives Residential a +2 bonus instead of +1, so a Park at the centre of an R cluster does double duty.

Road (*)

Infrastructure

Roads score along the row: each Road tile scores +1 for every connected Road in the same row. Build a straight, unbroken row of roads to make each segment count the whole run.

In Free Play, disconnected Road segments also cost 1 upkeep — a dead-end road is a liability twice over.

Controls, Saves & Leaderboard

Front Office
  • Place a building: click any highlighted valid cell, then choose a building type.
  • Demolish: select an occupied cell and confirm removal (costs 1 coin in Arcade and Challenges).
  • Bonus Tasks panel: in Arcade and Challenge, open the Bonus Tasks tab in-game to view active goals, completed goals, and batch progress.
  • Challenge event log: in Challenge mode, watch the event feed for warnings, active temporary effects, and disaster history.
  • Save: name your city and store the current board to continue later.
  • Load: pick a saved city from the list to resume it.
  • Leaderboard: when a game ends, if your score beats the lowest of the top 10 for that mode, you'll be asked to submit your name.
